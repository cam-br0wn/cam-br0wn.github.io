Cam Brown

I'm an SoC microarchitect at Ampere Computing in Portland, Oregon, where I'm a Senior Logic Design Engineer.

Most of my work is on the tools that take an architectural spec and turn it into a chip: integration flows that produce functionally and physically correct RTL, the floorplan that ships, and the power, timing, and area numbers the rest of the team plans around. I also own the CPU's SPR model.

Background

I joined Ampere in 2023 after interning there on the MMU of a custom core, and before that spent a year at SAS building test and triage automation for their machine learning platform.

I have an MS and a BS in Computer Engineering from Northwestern, with a focus on architecture and VLSI, and started out studying computer science and math at Carleton College.

Interests

Computer architecture, design automation, and lately agentic coding tools. Away from the keyboard: baseball, which I played through college, plus skiing, mountain biking, and climbing high peaks.
